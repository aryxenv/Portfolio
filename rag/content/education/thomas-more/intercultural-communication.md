# Intercultural Communication (YR0129)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Intercultural Communication` (Course Code: `YR0129`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y2Sem1`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **3 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **15/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y2Sem1 > InterculturalCommunication`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Intercultural Communication analyzes how cultural values, communication norms, and social conditioning influence international business operations, leadership styles, and cross-border negotiations.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

The theoretical core centers on Erin Meyer's *The Culture Map* across eight behavioral scales: Communicating (low-context vs. high-context), Evaluating (direct vs. indirect negative feedback), Persuading (principles-first vs. applications-first), Leading (egalitarian vs. hierarchical), Deciding (consensual vs. top-down), Trusting (task-based vs. relationship-based), Disagreeing (confrontational vs. avoids confrontation), and Scheduling (linear-time vs. flexible-time). It also reviews Geert Hofstede's cultural dimensions and Daniel Coyle's *The Culture Code*.

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Students analyzed multinational corporate case studies, participated in international business seminars (such as the GO PRO workshop and entrepreneurship seminars), and evaluated real-world cross-cultural communication breakdowns across team projects.

Ability to diagnose cross-cultural friction in international business, adapt communication and leadership styles to diverse cultural norms, and navigate multinational negotiations effectively.
