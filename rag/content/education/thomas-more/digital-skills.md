# Digital Skills (YB1202)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Digital Skills` (Course Code: `YB1202`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y2Sem1`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **3 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **17/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y2Sem1 > DigitalSkills`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Digital Skills refines high-level workplace productivity tools, focusing on advanced data modeling in Microsoft Excel and professional document typography in Microsoft Word.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

Theoretical topics encompass spreadsheet architecture, dynamic array formulas, lookup mechanics (INDEX/MATCH, XLOOKUP), conditional logic nesting, data validation rules, what-if analysis (Goal Seek, Scenario Manager), and structured document styling hierarchy for formal technical reports.

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Students engineered complex financial and operational spreadsheets across weekly lab exercises (`Week 4 excel start` through `Week 11`). Practical assignments involved multi-sheet data consolidation, building interactive Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts, dynamic lookup formulas, and authoring formal documentation adhering to corporate publishing standards.

## Folder Structure & Studied Materials

The course materials, assignments, codebases, and student deliverables are systematically archived in the university cloud storage directory under `OneDrive - Thomas More/Y2Sem1/DigitalSkills`. The preserved folder hierarchy includes:

- **`Week 2 - Week 3`**: Advanced Microsoft Word styling, hierarchical document outlines, citation management, and typographical conventions.
- **`Week 4 - Week 7`**: Microsoft Excel modeling: logical formulas, nested conditionals, dynamic arrays, and XLOOKUP integration.
- **`Week 8 - Week 11`**: Data aggregation with Pivot Tables, slicers, data validation, scenario analysis, and comprehensive practical assessments.

All code assets and technical reports reflect rigorous conceptual engineering, maintained without superfluous code dumps to preserve high-level semantic clarity.

## Key Competencies & Learning Outcomes

Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel for business analysis, building dynamic spreadsheet models, automating tabular lookups, and formatting executive-ready documentation.
