# Project Management (YB1308)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Project Management` (Course Code: `YB1308`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y3Sem1`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **3 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **12/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y3Sem1 > ProjectManagement`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Project Management teaches the governance structures, planning tools, and execution methodologies required to manage complex technical and business initiatives from inception to delivery.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

The course provides an in-depth comparative analysis between predictive (Waterfall, PRINCE2) and adaptive (Agile, Scrum, Kanban) project management frameworks. Topics include work breakdown structures (WBS), Gantt chart scheduling, critical path method (CPM), risk registers, stakeholder RACI matrices, user story estimation (story points, planning poker), and sprint burndown tracking.

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Coursework deliverables included two major group project investigations: the Agile Group Project (`Agile/Group_Assignment_Agile_Group 4.docx`) simulating Scrum sprint management, and the Waterfall Group Project (`Waterfall/Group_Assignment_Waterfall_Team4.docx`) formulating formal stage-gate project charters, WBS diagrams, and critical path schedules.

## Key Competencies & Learning Outcomes

Ability to lead projects using Agile Scrum and Waterfall frameworks, draft project charters and WBS schedules, manage risk registers, and align project teams with stakeholder timelines.
