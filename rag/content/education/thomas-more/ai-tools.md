# AI Tools (YB1400)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`AI Tools` (Course Code: `YB1400`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y2Sem1`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **3 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **16/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y2Sem1 > AItools`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

AI Tools explores the practical application of cutting-edge generative AI models and assistants within corporate environments, critically examining productivity gains alongside legal and regulatory risks.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

Theoretical topics include transformer architecture concepts, prompt engineering strategies (few-shot prompting, chain-of-thought), enterprise AI governance, European AI Act risk tiers, and GDPR data privacy considerations (preventing confidential data leakage, intellectual property rights, and AI bias).

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Students executed structured group investigations evaluating AI productivity tools for automated data cleaning and synthesis. Key deliverables included the comprehensive data protection assessment and case submission (`aryankarolis_DataProtection_Assignment.pdf` and `GDPRAI.docx`), auditing enterprise compliance risks when leveraging LLMs for sensitive business data processing.

## Folder Structure & Studied Materials

The course materials, assignments, codebases, and student deliverables are systematically archived in the university cloud storage directory under `OneDrive - Thomas More/Y2Sem1/AItools`. The preserved folder hierarchy includes:

- **`Generative AI Foundations`**: Overview of large language models, capabilities, multimodal interfaces, and enterprise use-cases.
- **`Prompt Engineering & Workflows`**: Structured prompt construction, chain-of-thought methodologies, and data cleaning automation.
- **`Data Protection & Group Assignment`**: In-depth research and formal report assessing GDPR compliance, data confidentiality, and risk mitigation in enterprise AI.

All code assets and technical reports reflect rigorous conceptual engineering, maintained without superfluous code dumps to preserve high-level semantic clarity.

## Key Competencies & Learning Outcomes

Ability to effectively prompt and deploy generative AI tools for productivity, critically audit AI systems for GDPR compliance risks, and establish data protection safeguards for AI usage.
