# Data Science Fundamentals (YP0820)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Data Science Fundamentals` (Course Code: `YP0820`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y2Sem1`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **6 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **16/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y2Sem1 > DataScience`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Data Science Fundamentals introduces formal statistical learning and supervised machine learning using Python, Scikit-Learn, and scientific computing packages. It grounds empirical data analysis in mathematical regression theory.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

The theoretical curriculum covers ordinary least squares (OLS) regression, loss functions (Mean Squared Error), gradient descent mechanics, assumptions of linear regression (homoscedasticity, multicollinearity, normality of residuals), polynomial feature expansion, bias-variance tradeoff, and regularized regression techniques including Lasso (L1 regularization) for feature selection and Ridge (L2 regularization) for shrinkage.

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Practical work was conducted across eleven structured Jupyter Notebook modules (`DSF 01` through `DSF 11`). Students implemented linear and polynomial regression models from scratch and via Scikit-Learn, evaluated model metrics ($R^2$, MAE, RMSE), diagnosed overfitting/underfitting, tuned regularization hyperparameter $\alpha$ in Lasso regression (`DSF 10 - Lasso Regression Exercise.ipynb`), and performed comprehensive model recap evaluations.

## Folder Structure & Studied Materials

The course materials, assignments, codebases, and student deliverables are systematically archived in the university cloud storage directory under `OneDrive - Thomas More/Y2Sem1/DataScience`. The preserved folder hierarchy includes:

- **`Week 1 - Week 3 (DSF 01 - 03)`**: Foundations of linear regression, univariate OLS fitting, loss minimization, and polynomial feature engineering.
- **`Week 4 - Week 6 (DSF 04 - 06)`**: Multiple linear regression, feature scaling (StandardScaler, MinMaxScaler), and multicollinearity diagnosis (VIF).
- **`Week 7 - Week 9 (DSF 07 - 09)`**: Model validation frameworks, train-test splitting, cross-validation, and residual analysis.
- **`Week 10 - Week 11 (DSF 10 - 11)`**: Regularization techniques (Lasso/L1, Ridge/L2), sparsity induction, hyperparameter tuning, and comprehensive recap.

All code assets and technical reports reflect rigorous conceptual engineering, maintained without superfluous code dumps to preserve high-level semantic clarity.

## Key Competencies & Learning Outcomes

Deep mathematical and practical understanding of regression algorithms, Scikit-Learn pipeline implementation, feature scaling, mitigating overfitting via L1/L2 regularization, and robust statistical model evaluation.
