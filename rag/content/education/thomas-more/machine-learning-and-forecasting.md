# Machine Learning & Forecasting (YP0853)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Machine Learning & Forecasting` (Course Code: `YP0853`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y3Sem1`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **6 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **19/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y3Sem1 > MachineLearning`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Machine Learning & Forecasting is an intensive 6-ECTS course taught by prominent Lecturer Bilal Özden, covering advanced supervised learning algorithms, ensemble architectures, and time-series forecasting. Aryan earned a **19/20 grade** and received an academic recommendation from Lecturer Özden.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

Theoretical topics encompass decision tree mechanics (entropy, Gini impurity, information gain, pruning), ensemble learning (Random Forests, bagging, Gradient Boosting), hyperparameter tuning (GridSearchCV, RandomizedSearchCV), feature selection, dimension reduction (PCA), data leakage prevention in pipelines, and time-series forecasting decomposition (trend, seasonality, holiday effects).

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Practical work spanned numerous Jupyter Notebook modules (`Machine-Learning---Forecasting/`). The capstone examination project was the **Auckland Cycling Forecasting Project** (`ml-exam-2025/Exam Assignment Part 2.ipynb`, `part_2_cycling_counts_by_day.csv`, `part_2_Auckland_weather.csv`), where Aryan engineered a sophisticated **Facebook Prophet** forecasting model predicting daily cycling volumes in Auckland by integrating multi-year weather indicators, day-of-week trends, and holiday effects.

## Folder Structure & Studied Materials

The course materials, assignments, codebases, and student deliverables are systematically archived in the university cloud storage directory under `OneDrive - Thomas More/Y3Sem1/MachineLearning`. The preserved folder hierarchy includes:

- **`Recap & Decision Trees (Modules 1 - 2)`**: Linear models recap, Decision Tree classifiers/regressors, and Random Forest ensemble architectures.
- **`Optimization & Evaluation (Modules 3 - 5)`**: Regularization, Cross-Validation, GridSearchCV, ROC/AUC metrics, and Principal Component Analysis (PCA).
- **`Data Leakage & Pipelines (Module 6)`**: Preventing data leakage across transformations, Scikit-Learn Pipeline architectures, and feature union.
- **`Auckland Cycling Forecasting Exam`**: Complete time-series forecasting pipeline using Facebook Prophet on Auckland cycling counts and weather telemetry.

All code assets and technical reports reflect rigorous conceptual engineering, maintained without superfluous code dumps to preserve high-level semantic clarity.

## Key Competencies & Learning Outcomes

High proficiency in Scikit-Learn pipelines, building Random Forest ensembles, preventing data leakage, and engineering advanced time-series forecasting models using Facebook Prophet.
