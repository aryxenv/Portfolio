# Management skills (YB1056)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Management skills` (Course Code: `YB1056`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y2Sem2`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **3 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **19/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y2Sem2 > ManagementSkills`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Management Skills develops the behavioral, organizational, and interpersonal competencies required to lead high-performing teams, resolve workplace friction, and guide organizational strategy.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

Core management theories cover organizational structures (functional, matrix, agile squads), leadership styles (situational leadership, transformational leadership), motivation theories (Herzberg's two-factor theory, Vroom's expectancy theory), strategic planning frameworks (OKRs, KPIs), delegating and feedback methodologies, and management practices in AI-transformed workplaces.

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Students tackled complex Problem-Based Learning (PBL) cases (such as the 'Houston, We Have a Problem' organizational crisis case). A major practical milestone was conducting an in-depth executive interview with a senior business manager at Belgian telecommunications leader **Telenet** (`managerInterview_msk5.docx`, `Interview_Telenet_structure_and_notes.docx`, and `miniReportInterview_aryan.docx`), analyzing real-world managerial decision-making, organizational agility, and leadership under pressure.

## Folder Structure & Studied Materials

The course materials, assignments, codebases, and student deliverables are systematically archived in the university cloud storage directory under `OneDrive - Thomas More/Y2Sem2/ManagementSkills`. The preserved folder hierarchy includes:

- **`Week 1 - Week 3`**: Principles of management, organizational design, strategic planning, and the 'Houston We Have a Problem' PBL case.
- **`Week 4 - Week 6`**: Team leadership dynamics, delegation models, active listening, conflict management, and constructive feedback loops.
- **`Week 7 - Week 10`**: Managing technological change, AI impact on workplace competencies, and guest seminar reflections ('Data for Good').
- **`Assignments & Executive Interview`**: Executive interview design, field interview execution with Telenet management, and final synthesis report.

All code assets and technical reports reflect rigorous conceptual engineering, maintained without superfluous code dumps to preserve high-level semantic clarity.

## Key Competencies & Learning Outcomes

Exceptional leadership communication, analyzing real-world organizational crises, conducting executive interviews, structuring agile management frameworks, and managing cross-functional technical teams.
