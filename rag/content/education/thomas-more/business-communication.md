# Business Communication (YB1351)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Business Communication` (Course Code: `YB1351`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y1Sem2`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **3 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **18/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y1Sem2 > BusinessCommunication`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Business Communication develops the executive verbal and written communication competencies necessary to operate effectively in global corporate environments. It aligns linguistic precision with strategic commercial storytelling.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

Theoretical topics encompass corporate communication channels, audience analysis, persuasive communication frameworks (Minto Pyramid Principle), formal corporate reporting registers, crisis communication fundamentals, and international business terminology anchored in the Pearson Business Partner B2 framework.

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Students authored formal commercial proposals, executed executive correspondence, and prepared strategic business pitch presentations. A central deliverable included the Lamborghini strategic corporate analysis and accompanying oral presentation script (`LamborghiniBC.pptx` and `lamborghiniScript.docx`), demonstrating professional argumentation, visual storytelling, and executive presence.

The course materials, assignments, codebases, and student deliverables are systematically archived in the university cloud storage directory under `OneDrive - Thomas More/Y1Sem2/BusinessCommunication`. The preserved folder hierarchy includes:

## Key Competencies & Learning Outcomes

High-level executive presentation fluency, structured written business correspondence, strategic argument formulation, and professional stakeholder engagement.
