# Identity & Access Management (YP0823)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Identity & Access Management` (Course Code: `YP0823`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y2Sem2`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **3 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **18/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y2Sem2 > IdentityAccessManagement`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Identity & Access Management (IAM) provides a deep technical dive into digital identity verification, delegated authorization, access governance, and post-quantum cryptographic security.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

The curriculum investigates identity lifecycle management, authentication vs. authorization, OAuth 2.0 grant types (Authorization Code Flow with PKCE, Client Credentials, Refresh Tokens), OpenID Connect (OIDC) ID tokens and userinfo endpoints, Access Control Models (Discretionary / DAC, Mandatory / MAC, Role-Based / RBAC, Attribute-Based / ABAC), Multi-Factor Authentication mechanisms (FIDO2, WebAuthn, TOTP), and Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) alongside post-quantum cryptography.

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Practical work included mapping token exchange flows in OAuth 2.0 and OIDC, configuring enterprise identity federation, delivering technical presentations on API security, and conducting specialized research on Quantum Cryptography (`QuantumCryptographyNotes.docx` and `QuantumCryptographyWithPeerEval.pdf`) examining quantum threat vectors to public-key infrastructure.

## Folder Structure & Studied Materials

The course materials, assignments, codebases, and student deliverables are systematically archived in the university cloud storage directory under `OneDrive - Thomas More/Y2Sem2/IdentityAccessManagement`. The preserved folder hierarchy includes:

- **`Week 1 - Week 3`**: Trust concepts, authentication fundamentals, symmetric/asymmetric cryptography, and digital signature verification.
- **`Week 4 - Week 6`**: OAuth 2.0 authorization framework: actors, endpoints, token types (JWT), and authorization code flows with PKCE.
- **`Week 7 - Week 9`**: OpenID Connect (OIDC) identity layer, RBAC/ABAC access control models, MFA enforcement, and Enterprise IAM directory federation.
- **`Assignments & Quantum Research`**: Specialized research on quantum cryptography threat implications and enterprise API identity architectures.

All code assets and technical reports reflect rigorous conceptual engineering, maintained without superfluous code dumps to preserve high-level semantic clarity.

## Key Competencies & Learning Outcomes

High technical competence in modern IAM protocols (OAuth 2.0, OIDC, JWT), architecting secure API authorization flows, implementing RBAC/ABAC policies, and evaluating cryptographic threats.
