# Governance & Risk Management Frameworks (YP0612)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Governance & Risk Management Frameworks` (Course Code: `YP0612`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y2Sem2`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **3 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **18/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y2Sem2 > GovernanceRisk`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Governance & Risk Management Frameworks teaches the strategic, legal, and operational governance structures required to safeguard enterprise assets against cybersecurity and compliance threats.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

Theoretical frameworks cover international security and governance standards, including ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (ISMS clauses 4 through 10 and Annex A control categories), the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 (Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover), COBIT, risk assessment methodologies (OCTAVE, FAIR, ISO 27005), threat likelihood and impact matrix quantification, and business continuity planning (BCP/DRP).

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

The primary practical deliverable was an extensive, professional Information Security Management System (ISMS) Review for **J.P. Morgan Chase & Co.** (`ISMS Final.docx` and `ISMS_final_jpmorgan.pdf`), prepared under Lecturer Bart Van Buitenen by Aryan Shah and team members Karolis Kalinauskas, Rustem Kamalidenov, Victor Hugo Maciel, and Kostyantin Dolyna. The report detailed organizational context, leadership commitments, asset inventories, quantitative risk assessments, and targeted Annex A mitigation controls for international financial systems.

## Folder Structure & Studied Materials

The course materials, assignments, codebases, and student deliverables are systematically archived in the university cloud storage directory under `OneDrive - Thomas More/Y2Sem2/GovernanceRisk`. The preserved folder hierarchy includes:

- **`Core Governance Standards`**: ISO/IEC 27001 architecture, NIST CSF, cybersecurity laws, and executive governance accountability.
- **`Risk Assessment Methodologies`**: Asset valuation, threat-vulnerability identification, quantitative vs. qualitative risk scoring, and treatment options.
- **`J.P. Morgan Chase ISMS Review`**: Comprehensive capstone ISMS audit deliverable evaluating J.P. Morgan's global banking infrastructure and compliance postures.

All code assets and technical reports reflect rigorous conceptual engineering, maintained without superfluous code dumps to preserve high-level semantic clarity.

## Key Competencies & Learning Outcomes

Mastery of enterprise information security governance, designing and auditing ISO 27001 ISMS frameworks, executing formal risk assessments, and drafting executive security compliance documentation.
