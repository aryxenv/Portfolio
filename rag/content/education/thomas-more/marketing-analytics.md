# Marketing Analytics (YP0845)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Marketing Analytics` (Course Code: `YP0845`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y2Sem2`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **3 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **16/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y2Sem2 > MarketingAnalytics`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Marketing Analytics connects data science methodologies with strategic commercial marketing, providing quantitative frameworks to understand customer behavior, segment audiences, and optimize digital acquisition funnels.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

Theoretical frameworks follow the four First Principles of Marketing Strategy: All Customers Differ (segmentation, targeting, positioning / STP), All Customers Change (customer lifecycle, CLV, transition matrices), All Competitors React (sustainable competitive advantage), and All Resources Are Limited (resource allocation). It covers k-means clustering, RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis, conversion funnels, and digital event tracking architectures.

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Hands-on labs used Python and Jupyter Notebooks (`UTF-8MarketingSegmentation.ipynb`). Students parsed transactional customer datasets, performed RFM segmentation, executed k-means clustering to define consumer personas, analyzed marketing case studies (such as the Pet Supply Store retail analysis), and configured event tracking schemas using Google Tag Manager (`Lesson 7 - Google Tag Manager.pdf`).

## Folder Structure & Studied Materials

The course materials, assignments, codebases, and student deliverables are systematically archived in the university cloud storage directory under `OneDrive - Thomas More/Y2Sem2/MarketingAnalytics`. The preserved folder hierarchy includes:

- **`Lesson 1 - Lesson 3`**: Introduction to marketing and digital analytics, online measurement models, and KPI framework formulation.
- **`Lesson 4 - Lesson 6`**: Marketing research methods, Principle 1 ('All Customers Differ') segmentation via clustering, and Principle 2 ('All Customers Change') CLV.
- **`Lesson 7 - Lesson 8`**: Google Tag Manager implementation, digital event tracking, privacy regulations (ePrivacy, GDPR), and consent mode.
- **`Assignments & Exam Prep`**: Customer segmentation Python pipelines, Pet Supply Store analytics case, and comprehensive exam preparation.

All code assets and technical reports reflect rigorous conceptual engineering, maintained without superfluous code dumps to preserve high-level semantic clarity.

## Key Competencies & Learning Outcomes

Proficiency in customer segmentation using k-means and RFM modeling, computing Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), implementing digital event tracking in Google Tag Manager, and data-driven marketing resource optimization.
