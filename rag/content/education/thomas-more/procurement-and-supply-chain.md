# Procurement & Supply Chain (YB0999)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Procurement & Supply Chain` (Course Code: `YB0999`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y2Sem2`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **3 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **13/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y2Sem2 > SupplyChainManagement`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Procurement & Supply Chain examines the strategic management of global logistics networks, sourcing strategies, inventory management models, and international regulatory compliance for circular supply chains.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

Theoretical concepts include strategic procurement matrices (Kraljic Matrix), total cost of ownership (TCO), supplier relationship management, inventory replenishment policies (EOQ, safety stock, JIT), GS1 global supply chain identification standards (GTIN, GLN, SSCC), operational excellence frameworks (Lean, Kaizen), and Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) legislation.

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Coursework deliverables included drafting a formal corporate procurement policy (`procurement-policy-r0984834.docx`), end-to-end supply chain process mapping of complex high-tech manufacturing (the iPhone global component process flow), solving the Fast Fashion delivery integrated logistics case (`Integrated Case_Deliver Fast Fashion_r0984834.docx`), and attending specialized guest lectures from GS1.

Ability to formulate corporate procurement policies, map global multi-tier supply chain workflows, evaluate inventory replenishment models, and align logistics with GS1 standards and EPR regulations.
