# Financial Management (YB1027)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Financial Management` (Course Code: `YB1027`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y2Sem1`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **6 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **16/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y2Sem1 > FinancialManagement`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Financial Management is a comprehensive 6-ECTS corporate finance course focusing on long-term capital allocation decisions, corporate valuation, risk-return dynamics, and corporate capital structures.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

Theoretical concepts cover the time value of money (discounting, compounding, annuities, perpetuities), capital budgeting criteria (Net Present Value, Internal Rate of Return, Payback Period, Profitability Index), cost of capital (Weighted Average Cost of Capital / WACC, Capital Asset Pricing Model / CAPM), working capital management strategies, and comprehensive financial statement ratio analysis (liquidity, leverage, coverage, turnover, and profitability ratios).

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Students solved complex multi-period investment appraisal models, authored formula sheets (`formulariumFM.docx`), completed chapter exercise sets across fifteen core topics (`CH1_exercises.docx` through `Ch15_Exercises.docx`), and conducted corporate financial health assessments evaluating real-world corporate balance sheets and valuation scenarios.

## Folder Structure & Studied Materials

The course materials, assignments, codebases, and student deliverables are systematically archived in the university cloud storage directory under `OneDrive - Thomas More/Y2Sem1/FinancialManagement`. The preserved folder hierarchy includes:

- **`Week 1 - Week 4 (Ch 1 - 5)`**: The role of managerial finance, financial statement analysis, ratio decomposition, and cash flow forecasting.
- **`Week 5 - Week 8 (Ch 6 - 9)`**: Time value of money, risk and return trade-offs, bond valuation, and equity pricing mechanisms.
- **`Week 9 - Week 12 (Ch 10 - 15)`**: Capital budgeting decision rules (NPV, IRR), project cash flow estimation, cost of capital (WACC), and leverage dynamics.
- **`GroupWork & Exam Prep`**: Integrated corporate valuation case studies, group problem sets, and comprehensive theoretical formula review.

All code assets and technical reports reflect rigorous conceptual engineering, maintained without superfluous code dumps to preserve high-level semantic clarity.

## Key Competencies & Learning Outcomes

Mastery of corporate financial modeling, executing discounted cash flow (DCF) analyses, computing WACC, evaluating capital investment proposals, and conducting in-depth financial ratio benchmarking.
