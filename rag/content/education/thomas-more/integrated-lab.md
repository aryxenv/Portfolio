# Integrated Lab (YP0932)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Integrated Lab` (Course Code: `YP0932`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y3Sem2`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **9 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **17/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y3Sem2 > special_olympics`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Integrated Lab is the 9-ECTS capstone multidisciplinary project in which students apply their cumulative expertise across business strategy, data science, cybersecurity, and full-stack software engineering to solve a real-world partner challenge for **Special Olympics Belgium (SOB)**. Aryan earned a **17/20 grade** for his solution.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

The project synthesized four integrated engineering disciplines: 1) Business Analysis (strategic positioning and operating model optimization for non-profit sports organizations), 2) Data Engineering & Analytics (ETL pipelines, data cleaning, and Power BI dashboards), 3) Information Security & GDPR (constructing Data Flow Diagrams, STRIDE threat modeling, and compliance reviews), and 4) Full-Stack Software Engineering (designing microservices, database schemas, and cost forecasting models).

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Coursework spanned five core pillars: 1) **Business Case** (`business_case/`): authoring the strategic business transformation proposal for SOB, 2) **Data Case** (`data_case/`): building analytics pipelines and interactive Power BI dashboards, 3) **Security Case** (`security_case/`): authoring the security threat report (`r0984834_Security_Report.docx`) and Data Flow Diagrams, 4) **SOB Hackathon** (`SOB_Hackathon/`): developing a full-stack application (`main.py`, FastAPI, Supabase, web UI) featuring an automated cost forecasting model (`app-cost_forcast/`), and 5) **Final Presentation** (`SOB_Hackathon_Presentation/`) pitching the validated platform to SOB leadership.
