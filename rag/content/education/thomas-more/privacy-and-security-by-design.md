# Privacy & Security By Design (YB1464)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Privacy & Security By Design` (Course Code: `YB1464`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y3Sem1`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **3 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **10/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y3Sem1 > SecurityByDesign`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Privacy & Security By Design teaches students how to proactively embed security and data privacy safeguards directly into system architectures from the initial design phase, rather than treating security as an afterthought.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

Theoretical principles cover the seven foundational Privacy by Design principles (Ann Cavoukian), Data Protection by Design and by Default (GDPR Article 25), the **STRIDE threat modeling methodology** (Spoofing, Tampering, Repudiation, Information Disclosure, Denial of Service, Elevation of Privilege), Data Flow Diagrams (DFDs), and attack surface reduction.

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

The primary practical deliverable was the **McHire Breach Threat Analysis & Mitigation Report** (`mcdonalds_report_or_something.docx` and `mcdonalds_case.pdf`), prepared by Aryan Shah and colleagues Sahir Canales, Karolis Kalinauskas, and Victor Maciel. The report dissected vulnerabilities in automated recruitment platforms, modeled threats across architectural data flows, and proposed robust defense mitigations alongside Salesforce enterprise case evaluations (`salesforce_case_v1.pptx`).

## Folder Structure & Studied Materials

The course materials, assignments, codebases, and student deliverables are systematically archived in the university cloud storage directory under `OneDrive - Thomas More/Y3Sem1/SecurityByDesign`. The preserved folder hierarchy includes:

- **`Core Security by Design Principles`**: Foundations of proactive security, privacy-by-design tenets, and architectural threat minimization.
- **`Threat Modeling Methodologies`**: STRIDE threat modeling, constructing Data Flow Diagrams (DFDs), and threat risk scoring.
- **`McHire Breach Capstone Deliverable`**: In-depth threat analysis of the McHire recruitment system breach, risk assessment, and technical mitigation architecture.

All code assets and technical reports reflect rigorous conceptual engineering, maintained without superfluous code dumps to preserve high-level semantic clarity.

## Key Competencies & Learning Outcomes

Ability to conduct architectural threat modeling using STRIDE, construct secure Data Flow Diagrams (DFDs), embed Privacy by Design controls into software, and formulate technical breach mitigation reports.
