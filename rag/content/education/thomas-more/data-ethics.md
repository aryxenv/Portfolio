# Data Ethics (YP0815)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Data Ethics` (Course Code: `YP0815`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y1Sem2`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **3 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **18/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y1Sem2 > DataProtection`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Data Ethics examines the intersection of technology, human rights, and legal jurisprudence. The course focuses on ethical obligations surrounding personal data processing, compliance with EU regulations, and responsible artificial intelligence deployment.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

Theoretical foundations center on the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / AVG), examining lawful processing grounds (consent, legitimate interest, contract performance), data subject rights (access, erasure, portability), data minimization, purpose limitation, storage limitation, Data Protection by Design and Default (DPbD), and Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIA). It also explores algorithmic bias, surveillance capitalism, and AI transparency.

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Coursework involved practical legal qualification exercises (`Exercise 7 - Qualification.docx`), GDPR compliance survey assessments, and authoring an extensive individual reflective portfolio (`dataEthicsReflection.docx` and `portfolio.docx`) evaluating real-world ethical dilemmas in facial recognition, commercial ad tracking, and automated credit scoring.

## Folder Structure & Studied Materials

The course materials, assignments, codebases, and student deliverables are systematically archived in the university cloud storage directory under `OneDrive - Thomas More/Y1Sem2/DataProtection`. The preserved folder hierarchy includes:

- **`DP1 - DP2`**: Foundations of digital ethics, philosophical frameworks (utilitarianism, deontology), and privacy rights.
- **`DP3 - DP4`**: GDPR legal architecture, data controller vs. processor roles, processing principles, and legal basis qualification.
- **`DP5 & Portfolio`**: Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs), ethical AI guidelines, and comprehensive personal ethical portfolio submission.

All code assets and technical reports reflect rigorous conceptual engineering, maintained without superfluous code dumps to preserve high-level semantic clarity.

## Key Competencies & Learning Outcomes

Ability to evaluate data architectures against GDPR compliance standards, identify ethical risks in data-driven systems, design privacy-preserving workflows, and compile formal DPIA assessments.
