# Entrepreneurial Management 1 (YB1004)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Entrepreneurial Management 1` (Course Code: `YB1004`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y1Sem1`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **3 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **15/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y1Sem1 > EntrepreneurialManagement`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Entrepreneurial Management 1 guides students through the early phases of the entrepreneurial lifecycle. It focuses on converting raw market insights into structured venture concepts, validating customer desirability, assessing financial viability, and articulating investor proposals.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

Core topics include opportunity recognition, the lean startup methodology, customer discovery interviews, competitive landscape mapping, unit economics, go-to-market strategies, and risk mitigation. Frameworks such as SWOT, PESTLE, and value-chain positioning are applied to early-stage ventures.

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Students analyzed detailed commercial cases including the Valet Bags venture case study and the Badlands case. The coursework culminated in authoring comprehensive entrepreneurial evaluation scripts, conducting financial breakeven analyses, and delivering persuasive executive pitch presentations supported by structured slide decks.
