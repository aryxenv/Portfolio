# Business Fundamentals (YB1316)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Business Fundamentals` (Course Code: `YB1316`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y1Sem1`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **3 ECTS Credits**.

- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y1Sem1 > BusinessFundamentals`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Business Fundamentals introduces the core structures of modern commercial enterprises, contrasting profit-driven corporations with non-profit entities. The course equips students with structural tools to map business models, deconstruct value creation, and assess strategic market positioning.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

The curriculum centers on Alexander Osterwalder's Business Model Canvas (BMC) and Value Proposition Canvas. Theoretical modules dissect each of the nine BMC building blocks—including customer segments, value propositions, channels, customer relationships, revenue streams, key resources, key activities, key partnerships, and cost structures. The course also explores W. Chan Kim and Renée Mauborgne's Blue Ocean Strategy, specifically the Eliminate-Reduce-Raise-Create (ERRC) grid, alongside effectuation theory for navigating entrepreneurial uncertainty and thriving in volatile market conditions.

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Practical coursework involved hands-on case evaluations applying BMC analysis to disruptive platforms such as Airbnb. Students executed an ERRC strategic canvas analysis on Cirque du Soleil to deconstruct how value innovation radically shifted industry boundaries. Additional individual reflection assignments assessed adaptability in dynamic, 'never normal' economic environments.

Students gain the ability to systematically formulate and evaluate commercial business models, map customer pain points to product value propositions, execute competitive differentiation via ERRC frameworks, and communicate business logic to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
