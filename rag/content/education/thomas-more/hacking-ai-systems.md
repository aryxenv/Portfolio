# Hacking AI Systems (YP0930)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Hacking AI Systems` (Course Code: `YP0930`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y3Sem1`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **3 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **17/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y3Sem1 > HackingAI`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Hacking AI Systems investigates the security vulnerabilities, attack surfaces, and threat mitigation strategies unique to Artificial Intelligence and Large Language Model architectures.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

The theoretical curriculum covers adversarial machine learning attack taxonomies (evasion, poisoning, model inversion, membership inference), the **OWASP Top 10 for Large Language Models** (Prompt Injection, Insecure Output Handling, Training Data Poisoning, Model Denial of Service, Supply Chain Vulnerabilities), the MITRE ATT&CK and ATLAS (Adversarial Threat Landscape for Artificial-Intelligence Systems) frameworks, and AI threat intelligence.

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Students mapped real-world advanced persistent threats using the MITRE ATT&CK Navigator (`group_case_attack_navigator/`, `andariel.json`, `apt38.json`). The central case study was the **Bank Fraud AI** security review (`Bank Fraud AI Final.pdf`), analyzing vulnerabilities in automated banking fraud detection models and proposing defense-in-depth countermeasures against adversarial perturbation.

## Folder Structure & Studied Materials

The course materials, assignments, codebases, and student deliverables are systematically archived in the university cloud storage directory under `OneDrive - Thomas More/Y3Sem1/HackingAI`. The preserved folder hierarchy includes:

- **`Threat Intelligence & Resilience`**: Cyber threat landscapes, intelligence-led defense, threat actor profiling (APT38, Andariel), and ATT&CK Navigator.
- **`OWASP Top 10 for LLMs`**: Deep dive into LLM vulnerabilities: direct/indirect prompt injection, model jailbreaking, and data leakage.
- **`Bank Fraud AI Security Review`**: Capstone case study analyzing adversarial attack vectors against machine learning banking models and designing robust mitigations.

All code assets and technical reports reflect rigorous conceptual engineering, maintained without superfluous code dumps to preserve high-level semantic clarity.

## Key Competencies & Learning Outcomes

Ability to conduct AI threat modeling, identify and remediate OWASP Top 10 LLM vulnerabilities, analyze adversarial machine learning risks, and utilize MITRE ATT&CK/ATLAS matrices for threat profiling.
