# Corporate Social Responsibility (YB1319)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Corporate Social Responsibility` (Course Code: `YB1319`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y3Sem1`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **3 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **12/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y3Sem1 > CorporateSocialResponsibility`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) examines the environmental, ethical, and social governance obligations of contemporary corporations, balancing commercial profitability with ecological stewardship.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

Theoretical modules analyze Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) rating criteria, the EU Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD), circular economy models, stakeholder theory, carbon accounting (Scopes 1, 2, and 3 emissions), and ethical supply chain auditing.

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Practical work included authoring an in-depth corporate sustainability analysis on **Porsche AG** (`CSR_porsche.docx`), examining Porsche's Strategy 2030, sustainable performance sports car manufacturing, electrification roadmap, e-fuels research, and battery recycling loops. Coursework also included personal community volunteer work reflections (`Volunteer_Work_Reflection_Questions.docx`).

Understanding of ESG compliance standards, evaluating corporate sustainability strategies, identifying circular economy opportunities, and conducting stakeholder impact assessments.
