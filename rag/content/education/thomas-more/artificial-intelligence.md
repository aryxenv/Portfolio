# Artificial Intelligence (YP0865)

`Artificial Intelligence` (Course Code: `YP0865`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus.

Artificial Intelligence covers algorithmic search, game theory, heuristic optimization, and machine intelligence paradigms. Aryan achieved a **perfect score of 20/20** in this advanced course.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

Theoretical concepts include state-space search (uninformed vs. informed search, A* algorithm, admissibility, consistency), adversarial search in zero-sum games (Minimax algorithm, Alpha-Beta pruning, transposition tables, move ordering), evaluation heuristics, Efficiently Updatable Neural Networks (NNUE) for rapid positional evaluation, decision trees, and foundational neural networks.

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

The flagship project developed in this course was Aryan's **Chess AI / NNUE Chessbot** and automated **Lichess Bot** (`lichess-bot/`, `lichess-bot.py`, `homemade.py`). Aryan engineered a high-performance evaluation engine utilizing Alpha-Beta pruning, quiescent search, custom positional evaluation weights, and automated Lichess server game loop handlers (`extra_game_handlers.py`), deploying an autonomous bot competing against human and AI players on the Lichess platform.
