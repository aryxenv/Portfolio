# Introduction to Financial Management (YB1317)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Introduction to Financial Management` (Course Code: `YB1317`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y1Sem2`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **3 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **15/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y1Sem2 > FinancialManagement`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Introduction to Financial Management provides foundational financial literacy, enabling students to interpret corporate financial statements, understand capital allocation, and assess business solvency and profitability.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

Core topics include the fundamental accounting equation (`Assets = Liabilities + Equity`), double-entry bookkeeping mechanisms, balance sheet components (current/fixed assets, short/long-term liabilities, equity), income statement (profit and loss) structure, revenue recognition, depreciation schedules, operational cash flow vs. net profit, and basic liquidity and solvency ratios.

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Students analyzed real-world corporate balance sheets and income statements, computed working capital metrics, evaluated inventory valuation impacts, and authored comprehensive examination summaries (`ExamSummary.docx`) solving practical financial assessment scenarios.

The course materials, assignments, codebases, and student deliverables are systematically archived in the university cloud storage directory under `OneDrive - Thomas More/Y1Sem2/FinancialManagement`. The preserved folder hierarchy includes:

- **`FM4 - FM6`**: Profit & loss statements, revenue and expense recognition, operating costs, and depreciation methodologies.
- **`FM7 - FM8`**: Cash flow statement analysis, working capital requirements, introductory liquidity ratios, and final exam synthesis.

All code assets and technical reports reflect rigorous conceptual engineering, maintained without superfluous code dumps to preserve high-level semantic clarity.

## Key Competencies & Learning Outcomes

Ability to read and interpret balance sheets, income statements, and cash flow reports, assess short-term liquidity and solvency, and evaluate corporate financial health.
