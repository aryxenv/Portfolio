# Business Intelligence Lab (YB1345)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Business Intelligence Lab` (Course Code: `YB1345`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y3Sem1`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **3 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **14/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y3Sem1 > BusinessIntelligenceLab`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Business Intelligence Lab focuses on high-level analytical strategy, teaching students to transition from reactive, metric-heavy reporting to proactive, Decision-Driven Analytics (DDA) that directly inform strategic business choices.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

The course is anchored in Bart De Langhe and Stefano Puntoni's *Decision-Driven Analytics* methodology. It critically examines the pitfalls of data-driven decision-making (metric obsession, data deluge, confirmation bias) and teaches students to reverse the analytical flow: identifying the decision to be made, defining decision alternatives, formulating required information, and designing analytical models to evaluate those specific options.

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Students analyzed complex corporate scenarios including the **Synapcom** e-commerce logistics case study (`decision_driven_analytics_synapcom.pdf`), created conceptual decision mindmaps, evaluated executive dashboard navigation, and authored structured decision alternative matrices (`context_decision_alternatives.pdf`) evaluating real-world business trade-offs.

## Folder Structure & Studied Materials

The course materials, assignments, codebases, and student deliverables are systematically archived in the university cloud storage directory under `OneDrive - Thomas More/Y3Sem1/BusinessIntelligenceLab`. The preserved folder hierarchy includes:

- **`Week 1 - Week 2`**: Decision-Driven Analytics principles, conceptual mindmapping, and navigating executive dashboards.
- **`Week 3 - Week 5`**: Applying DDA to marketing and corporate strategy, identifying alternative scenarios, and data storytelling.
- **`Synapcom Case Assignment`**: Complete strategic decision-driven analysis for Synapcom, mapping operational bottlenecks to decision alternatives.

All code assets and technical reports reflect rigorous conceptual engineering, maintained without superfluous code dumps to preserve high-level semantic clarity.

## Key Competencies & Learning Outcomes

Ability to apply Decision-Driven Analytics frameworks, design dashboards oriented around concrete business decisions, mitigate cognitive bias in reporting, and synthesize analytical insights into executive recommendations.
