# Personal Development (YB1002)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Personal Development` (Course Code: `YB1002`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y1Sem2`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **3 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **16/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y1Sem2 > PersonalDevelopment`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Personal Development guides students through evidence-based self-evaluation, academic habit formation, emotional intelligence, and long-term career planning within the technology and business sectors.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

Theoretical frameworks include the GROW coaching model (Goal, Reality, Options, Will), the LASSI (Learning and Study Strategies Inventory) assessment framework, self-determination theory (autonomy, competence, relatedness), time management matrix (Eisenhower Matrix), and reflective learning cycles (Gibbs' Reflective Cycle).

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Deliverables included diagnostic LASSI assessments tracking study habit improvements across semesters, active participation in external business seminars, and authoring comprehensive reflective essays (`Final Assignment GROW.docx`, `growModelFinal.docx`, and `Final reflection-2-2.docx`) charting career milestones, technical skill acquisition paths, and personal development goals.

## Key Competencies & Learning Outcomes

High self-awareness, proficiency with structured reflection frameworks (GROW), proactive goal setting, adaptive time management, and professional career roadmap design.
