# Programming Advanced (YP0847)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Programming Advanced` (Course Code: `YP0847`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y2Sem1`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **6 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **19/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y2Sem1 > ProgrammingAdvanced`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Programming Advanced is a rigorous 6-ECTS computer science course focusing on algorithmic design paradigms, complex data structures, and algorithmic complexity analysis in C#.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

Theoretical topics include asymptotic analysis (Big-O, Big-Omega, Big-Theta runtime and space complexity), recursive mathematical formulation and call-stack dynamics, divide-and-conquer algorithms (QuickSort, MergeSort), dynamic programming principles (memoization, tabulation, optimal substructure, overlapping subproblems), backtracking state-space trees, and non-linear data structures (trees, binary search trees, graphs, adjacency matrices/lists, BFS, DFS).

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Students implemented advanced algorithms in clean C# codebases. Notable solutions included dynamic programming implementations for Rod Cutting optimization (`RodCutting.cs`) and Fibonacci memoization (`Fibonacci.cs`), backtracking algorithms for the classic N-Queens puzzle (`Queens.cs`) and 2D Maze traversal (`Maze.cs`), recursive Towers of Hanoi simulations (`TowersOfHanoi.cs`), and combinatorial subset generation.

## Folder Structure & Studied Materials

The course materials, assignments, codebases, and student deliverables are systematically archived in the university cloud storage directory under `OneDrive - Thomas More/Y2Sem1/ProgrammingAdvanced`. The preserved folder hierarchy includes:

- **`Week 2 - Week 4`**: Recursion fundamentals, call-stack visualization, divide-and-conquer, QuickSort partitioning, and recursive math.
- **`Week 5 - Week 7`**: Dynamic programming: memoization vs. tabulation, optimal substructure, Fibonacci, and the classic Rod Cutting problem.
- **`Week 8 - Week 10`**: Backtracking algorithms, state-space exploration, N-Queens problem, and maze search pathfinding.
- **`Week 11 - Week 13`**: Graph representations, depth-first and breadth-first search traversals, algorithmic optimization, and comprehensive examination.

All code assets and technical reports reflect rigorous conceptual engineering, maintained without superfluous code dumps to preserve high-level semantic clarity.

## Key Competencies & Learning Outcomes

Exceptional algorithmic problem-solving in C#, mastery of recursive and dynamic programming optimization, implementing backtracking search algorithms, and conducting rigorous computational complexity analysis.
