# Databases (YP0577)

## Course Overview & Academic Context

`Databases` (Course Code: `YP0577`) is a mandatory course unit in the Bachelor of International Business Management (Data Science, Protection & Security) curriculum at Thomas More University of Applied Sciences, Mechelen Campus. 

- **Academic Period**: Completed during academic semester **`Y1Sem1`**.
- **Credit Weight**: **6 ECTS Credits**.
- **Official Examination Result**: **18/20** (Pass with credit obtained).
- **Academic Archive Location**: `OneDrive - Thomas More > Y1Sem1 > Databases`.
- **Authoritative Source**: Confirmed in official Thomas More Transcript of Records (Code: `09C6AF65875C4383AC5A581FFEFEB9B0`).

Databases establishes rigorous proficiency in relational data architecture, relational algebra, and Structured Query Language (SQL). Students learn to translate real-world business requirements into normalized relational models and query multi-table schemas with high performance.

## Core Curriculum & Theoretical Foundations

The theoretical curriculum covers relational data models, entity-relationship modeling (entities, attributes, primary keys, foreign keys, cardinality, and participation constraints), functional dependencies, and relational normalization through Third Normal Form (1NF, 2NF, 3NF). It explores database constraints (unique, check, referential integrity), index mechanics, ACID transaction properties, and relational algebra operations (selection, projection, Cartesian product, joins).

## Practical Lab Work & Hands-On Projects

Extensive lab coursework involved authoring Data Definition Language (DDL) and Data Manipulation Language (DML) scripts. Students engineered schemas for HR, inventory, invoicing, and store retail operations (`create-db-store.sql`, `create-db-hr.sql`, etc.), modeled complex business rules into ERDs, and executed advanced queries across the classic Northwind enterprise dataset utilizing multi-table inner/outer joins, nested subqueries, grouping, aggregations, and window functions.

## Folder Structure & Studied Materials

The course materials, assignments, codebases, and student deliverables are systematically archived in the university cloud storage directory under `OneDrive - Thomas More/Y1Sem1/Databases`. The preserved folder hierarchy includes:

- **`01Databases - 03Databases`**: Relational database introduction, entity identification, relational attributes, and conceptual ERD modeling.
- **`04Databases - 06Databases`**: Relational normalization principles (1NF to 3NF), DDL table creation, foreign key constraints, and schema migration.
- **`07Databases - 09Databases`**: Advanced DML query engineering, aggregate functions, filtering, subqueries, and multi-table join strategies.
- **`10Databases - 12Databases`**: Enterprise case studies using Northwind and multi-domain retail schemas, performance tuning, and final practical exams.

All code assets and technical reports reflect rigorous conceptual engineering, maintained without superfluous code dumps to preserve high-level semantic clarity.

## Key Competencies & Learning Outcomes

Mastery of conceptual and logical database design, schema normalization, constraint enforcement, writing robust SQL queries for complex reporting, and diagnosing relational schema anti-patterns.
