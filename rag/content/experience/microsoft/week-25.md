   - *Challenge*: Multi-column architecture scoring matrices in the AI solution catalog caused horizontal scrolling and visual truncation on smartphone screens.
   - *Decision*: Implemented responsive card transformations that automatically convert wide tabular rows into vertical collapsible cards on viewport widths below 768px.
